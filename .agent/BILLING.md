# Invoice, payment and collection

## Agreed rules
- One consolidated invoice per customer per wholly completed auction. A final winning line is a purchase immediately; until the complete auction is closed and invoice processing succeeds, its status is Vundet.
- Hammer price is for the entire auction line, regardless of quantity. Buyer premium is 10% of that price. Both amounts include 25% VAT; VAT is extracted, never added again. Each line/component rounds to two decimals, midpoint away from zero; invoice totals sum saved line amounts. Example: hammer 1,000 DKK, premium 100 DKK, total 1,100 DKK, included VAT 220 DKK.
- Manual full payment only, recorded as Card or Cash. No payment deadline was supplied, so none is invented. All Employee, LotAdmin, AuctionAdmin and Admin accounts may register payment and collection. No external payment provider, charge, subscription, invoice email or messaging is implemented.
- Full invoice payment is required before collection. Entire auction lines may then be collected separately; individual physical units within one line are collected together. A line payment receipt becomes available after full invoice payment.
- Invoice number: AH{auction number}-{dd-MM-yyyy}-{global counter:D6}, e.g. AH42-29-10-2026-000001. Date is the issuance date in Europe/Copenhagen. Both counters start at 1 and never reset; six digits is minimum padding, not a limit. Auction business number is allocated on first successful invoicing; auctions without invoiced wins retain null. This does not alter the existing auction UUID.

## Persistence and retries
- AuctionInvoice/AuctionInvoiceLine store immutable issued snapshots of seller CVR/name/address/email, buyer name/address/email, auction/item names, description, quantity, winning bid, close time, prices, rates and tax amounts. APIs provide no amendment/deletion route for issued values.
- AuctionInvoiceWorker scans on startup and every five seconds, independently of RabbitMQ, and retries missing invoices using fresh scopes. A failure for one auction does not stop the remaining auctions. Discovery is a hint: the store rechecks whole-auction completion inside the transaction.
- PostgreSQL uses the existing auction/stock advisory lock before the auction row lock. Counters, header, all customer invoices and lines commit together. Unique indexes cover customer/auction, source line, winning bid, invoice number/sequence and auction number. Payment/collection use the same transaction-scoped advisory lock before loading current state.
- Payment records total, method, UTC time and authenticated staff login UserId/email snapshot. Collection records UTC time and that staff identity per line. These are login identities, not an invented link to the separate Employee catalogue. Exact repeated payments/collections preserve the original audit; changed payment method/amount is rejected.
- Customer queries derive customer identity from the persisted login link and filter before returning invoice, receipt or purchase history. Invoiced purchase history uses saved invoice-line values. Staff listing and mutations require the staff role policy on the server.

## Operation and verification
- Apply 20261029123907_AddAuctionInvoices through the existing deployment migration process. The previous Invoice table is preserved: it cannot represent consolidated invoices or historical monetary details. If an auction already has legacy Invoice rows, automatic invoicing fails explicitly and requires reconciliation; it does not issue a second invoice or guess old terms.
- Billing:VatPercent is 25 in API appsettings.json. Missing or invalid VAT configuration causes invoicing to fail/retry instead of silently choosing a rate. Already issued invoices retain their saved rate and amounts.
- UI: /kunde/mine-koeb, /kunde/fakturaer/{id}, /kunde/fakturaer/{id}/kvittering/{lineId}; staff: /admin/invoices. Documents are displayed in the authenticated app; nothing is sent automatically.
- Automated coverage includes inclusive tax/rounding, whole-line pricing, real PostgreSQL winning bid and stock closure, concurrent issuance/payment/collection, rollback/retry, multiple customers/auctions, database uniqueness, legacy invoice protection, customer/staff authorization, fresh-host persistence, historical edits and rendered invoice/receipt pages.
- PostgreSQL tests create/drop only disposable randomly named databases when AUCTION_STOCK_TEST_CONNECTION is set. The migration test applies the previous migration, inserts seller data, upgrades, verifies preservation and checks model parity. Live browser acceptance and deployment against the intended database remain separate checks.
